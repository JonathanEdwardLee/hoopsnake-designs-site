Launch System first

What the Launch System includes — and what stays separate.

Standard Launch System scope
  • Custom one-page or small-site architecture appropriate to the project
  • Mobile-first responsive design with performance and accessibility in mind
  • Technical SEO and privacy foundation
  • Qualified project-review form
  • One bounded integration
  • Two consolidated revision rounds
  • Explicit handoff documentation
  • Proposed 14-day launch-defect correction window
Separate scopes
  • Larger apps, games, commerce platforms, or backends
  • Ongoing marketing retainers or open-ended support
  • Public AI chat or visitor-content processing
  • Payment processing or recurring platform expansion

Payment milestones are discussed in proposals — not published here as binding policy.

Boundaries / FAQ

Clear scope beats vague promises.

Is this a template install?

No. The page itself is meant to prove design ability — authored, premium, and memorable rather than generic agency filler.

Do you publish payment milestones here?

No. Larger apps, commerce, backends, ongoing marketing, and open-ended support are scoped separately in proposals.

What about AI chat?

V1 does not include public AI chat or send visitor project content to any AI provider.

What happens after I submit?

Submission received means the inquiry is in review. Jonathan evaluates fit before scheduling a call and does not promise acceptance.
