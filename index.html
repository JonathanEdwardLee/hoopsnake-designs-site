What I Build

Websites, apps, and tools with a clear job.

The work stays specific. If a project needs something larger than a focused build, that gets its own conversation and proposal.

Websites

Custom one-page and small-site work with clear structure, mobile-first layout, and room for the project’s actual voice.

Web apps

Focused product interfaces when the job, audience, and ownership are clear enough to build against.

Mobile apps

How I Work

Review. Conversation. Proposal. Build.

  1. 01Review

    Send the project form when you're ready. I read it and reply about next steps.

  2. 02Conversation

    If the project looks like a good fit, we'll have a focused conversation about goals, constraints, and who decides.

  3. 03Proposal

    I'll put scope, timeline, deliverables, and investment in writing before I start building.

  4. 04Build + Launch

    I implement, review, hand off, and launch. Larger apps and ongoing support get a separate scope.
