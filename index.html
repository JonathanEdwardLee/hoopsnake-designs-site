What I Build

Websites, apps, and tools with a clear job.

The work stays specific. If a project needs something larger than a focused build, that gets its own conversation and proposal.

Websites

Custom one-page and small-site work with clear structure, mobile-first layout, and room for the project’s actual voice.

Web apps

Focused product interfaces when the job, audience, and ownership are clear enough to build against.

Mobile apps

Native and mobile-product work when a phone is the right place for the tool, not just a shrunk website.

Interactive experiences

Character-led, game-like, or otherwise unusual pages that still have to load, read, and work.

Custom tools / integrations

Bounded workflow tools, publishing systems, and one well-chosen integration instead of a pile of extras.

About

You work with Jonathan.

Hoopsnake Designs is Jonathan Edward Lee’s design and development studio. He designs and builds websites, apps, and digital tools for businesses, creative projects, and people who want authored work instead of a template.

There is no account team in between. Inquiries are read by Jonathan. Fit is checked before a call is scheduled, and a proposal is written before build starts.

How We'll Work Together

Review. Conversation. Proposal. Build.

  1. 01Review

    Send the project form when you are ready. Jonathan reads it asynchronously and checks fit before any scheduling.

  2. 02Conversation

    If the work looks aligned, a focused conversation clarifies goals, constraints, and who decides.

  3. 03Proposal

    Scope, timeline, deliverables, and investment are written down before build begins. Acceptance is not assumed.

  4. 04Build + Launch

    Implementation, review rounds, handoff, and launch. Larger apps and ongoing support stay on a separate scope.
